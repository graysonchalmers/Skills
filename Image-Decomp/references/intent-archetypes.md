# Engagement Archetype Library (extensible)

A shared vocabulary for the **intent axis**. One library covers both worlds:
a game key-art's "Power Fantasy" and an influencer's "Aspirational Flex" are
listed side by side because they pull the same lever. Match 1–4 archetypes per
image, each with a **confidence 0.0–1.0** and the specific in-image signals that
justify it. **The library is a starting set, not a cage** — if a real pattern
isn't here, name it, describe its signals, and score it. Add durable new ones
back into this file.

Organize your read by the four **intent axes** below; an image often sits on
more than one.

---

## Axis 1 — Desire / Aspiration  (make the viewer WANT)

**Power Fantasy** — viewer is invited to *become* a dominant, capable figure.
Signals: low camera angle, hero backlight, weapon/gear at peak readiness, frozen
action apex, scale dominance over environment. *Home turf: game key-art, sports,
trucks, fitness.*

**Aspirational Flex** — "this life could be yours." Signals: status locations
(infinity pool, business class), luxury goods framed casually, effortless-looking
wealth, golden light. *Home turf: influencer lifestyle, travel, luxury ads.*

**Thirst / Sexual Draw** — bodily allure is the primary hook. Signals: emphasized
body framing, direct or coy eye contact, skin/lip highlight, pose engineered for
the camera while performing "just existing." *Rate honestly and clinically; note
when it's disguised as something else (fitness, fashion, "art").*

**Cozy Grounding / Belonging** — "this could be your calm, safe, warm life."
Signals: soft warm light, domestic comfort props (coffee, blankets, pets),
soft focus, non-threatening framing, muted palette. *Home turf: hygge lifestyle,
cozy-game marketing, home brands.*

---

## Axis 2 — Social / Tribal  (locate the viewer in a group)

**Tribal Achievement / Prestige** — "winners look like this; join them."
Signals: trophies, badges, ranked/exclusive settings, uniform of a desirable
in-group, before/after transformation. *Home turf: fitness, finance, esports,
credential-flex posts.*

**Relatability Signal** — deliberate imperfection to build parasocial trust.
Signals: "messy bun," no-makeup-makeup, cluttered-but-curated background, bloopers
framing, self-deprecating caption over a still-flattering image. *The imperfection
is itself a technique — flag the gap between "flaw shown" and "flaw real."*

**In-group Coding** — subcultural signifiers that reward insiders and filter
outsiders. Signals: genre iconography, niche fashion, references only the tribe
reads, aesthetic "dog whistles." *Home turf: subculture brands, fandom art,
scene photography.*

---

## Axis 3 — Tension / Attention  (seize the eye NOW)

**Threat / Stakes Setup** — danger or conflict promises payoff. Signals: looming
antagonist, environmental hazard, weapon drawn, unresolved peril, high contrast +
cool/red danger palette. *Home turf: thriller/horror keys, action-game promo.*

**Curiosity Gap / Pattern-Interrupt** — "wait, what is happening?" withholds
resolution to force a click. Signals: obscured face, impossible/absurd juxtaposition,
mid-action ambiguity, an arrow/circle pointing at a hidden thing. *Home turf:
YouTube thumbnails, clickbait, viral stills.*

**FOMO / Scarcity** — urgency and exclusivity. Signals: "last chance" framing,
limited-drop staging, a crowd/queue, timestamped or event-locked context. *Home
turf: product drops, event marketing.*

---

## Axis 4 — Trust / Authenticity  (manage how "real" it feels)

**Authenticity Performance** — constructed content dressed as spontaneous/candid.
Signals: "caught mid-laugh" that's clearly directed, natural-look studio light,
retouching under a candid caption, impossible-luck framing. *This axis feeds the
Authenticity Gap score directly — high performance = high gap.*

**Authority / Credibility Signal** — "trust me, I'm the expert." Signals: clinical
white, lab/office/tool context, clean symmetry, muted authoritative palette,
diploma/instrument props. *Home turf: skincare, finance, health, B2B.*

**Native-Ad Mimicry** — advertising disguised as organic content. Signals: ad
that copies the platform's native post format, "just sharing" tone over a product
hero, missing/soft disclosure. *Feeds Construction Transparency = low (disguised).*

---

## How to apply

1. Scan all four axes; most images hit 1–3 archetypes across 1–2 axes.
2. For each, cite the **actual signals in THIS image** — never assert an archetype
   without evidence. "Power Fantasy 0.85 — low angle + backlit hero + raised
   weapon at action apex."
3. Feed the read into the two scores:
   - **Dopamine Hook** rises with Tension-axis archetypes and salience mechanics.
   - **Authenticity Gap** rises with Authenticity Performance / Relatability
     Signal on `claims-real` media.
4. Keep it descriptive. A key-art SHOULD run Power Fantasy at 0.9 — that's good
   craft, not a crime. Name the lever; let the reader judge the ethics.
